RhymASL introduces a playful storytelling interactive tool that highlights phonological features of ASL signs. The system generates interactive rhyming ASL stories that families can watch and shape together, encouraging ASL interactions and building phonological awareness [ASSETS’ 25](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3663547.3759734).
