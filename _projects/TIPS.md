Tabletop Interactive Play System (TIPS) observes parent–child interaction through a camera and microphone, then uses a projector to display relevant ASL signs retrieved from multiple sign language libraries. This line of work investigates how AI can offer context-responsive ASL recommendations and balance usefulness with autonomy in parent-child ASL interactions ([ASSETS’23](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3597638.3614511), [IDC’25](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3713043.3727054)).
